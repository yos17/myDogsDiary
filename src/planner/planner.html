🐸 About the ABCDE Method

The ABCDE method is a powerful prioritization system from Brian Tracy's bestselling book "Eat That Frog!" It helps you focus on what truly matters.

A

A - Must Do

Critical tasks with serious consequences if not done. These are your "frogs" - eat them first! Examples: project deadlines, important meetings, urgent problems.

B

B - Should Do

Important tasks with mild consequences. Do these after your A tasks. Examples: returning calls, routine reports, minor issues.

C

C - Nice to Do

Tasks with no consequences. Do these only after A and B tasks are complete. Examples: coffee with a colleague, checking personal email.

D

D - Delegate

Tasks that someone else can do. Free up your time for A and B tasks. Examples: routine tasks, administrative work that others can handle.

E

E - Eliminate

Tasks that don't need to be done at all. Learn to say no! Examples: time wasters, unnecessary meetings, busywork.

"The hardest part of any important task is getting started on it in the first place."
